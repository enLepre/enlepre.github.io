Does stimulating a catalytic reaction use less energy than heating it? Thermal Efficiency compares the energy consumed by light, electricity, mechanical stress, ultrasound, or a custom stimulus with the energy needed to obtain the same rate enhancement through heating.

Enter the stimulus power, operating time, observed rate enhancement, solvent, and volume. If the thermal activation energy is unknown, the tool finds the break-even threshold and the range where stimulation uses less energy. If it is known, the calculator gives the equivalent temperature, heating energy, and stimulated-to-thermal energy ratio.

Choose an ideal comparison or include equipment efficiencies, heat losses, and auxiliary loads. Solvent presets and editable properties help define the system; plots show how the comparison changes with activation energy and operating time. Scenarios can be shared by link or exported as CSV.

This is a screening model for a batch liquid with a comparable thermal pathway. Matching the rate enhancement does not establish equal yield, selectivity, or mechanism. The tool reports when the equivalent temperature falls outside its physical or user-defined limits.
