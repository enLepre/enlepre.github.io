At what cell voltage does electricity become cheaper than a chemical reagent for a redox process? Electron Cost puts both routes on the same basis, in USD per mole of useful electron equivalents, and plots the comparison as the cell voltage changes.

Choose a reagent and an electricity source, or enter your own prices. Adjust the cell voltage and efficiencies to see how the break-even point moves. The calculator includes a sources table, an explanation of the assumptions, and options to share a scenario or export it as CSV.

The comparison covers reagent and electricity consumption. A full process comparison would also account for factors such as equipment, separation, and waste.
